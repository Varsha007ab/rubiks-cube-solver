RUBIK'S CUBE VISION SOLVER
===========================

Sample Output

Input:
Six images representing the six faces of a 3×3 Rubik's Cube.

Processing:
1. Load cube face images
2. Detect 3×3 sticker grid
3. Convert image to HSV
4. Classify sticker colors
5. Validate cube configuration
6. Generate Kociemba cube string
7. Solve cube
8. Display solution moves
